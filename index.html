Microphone Recorder privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The extension processes microphone audio on your device. It does not upload your recordings or require an account.

What stays on your device

Your recordings, file names, durations and recording preferences are stored in your Chrome profile. When Chrome completes a download, it saves a separate copy on your device. Removing the extension can delete its local archive; downloaded copies are separate. Mic test samples are temporary and aren’t added to your archive or downloaded.

Network requests and pages

Recording, playback and export do not send your audio over the network. The extension can open a welcome page after installation and an optional feedback page after removal. If you choose an option in the in-app feedback prompt, it can open a feedback page or the Chrome Web Store. These pages are separate from the recorder. They use Umami to measure page visits; the analytics script does not receive recordings from the extension. If you choose to submit feedback or a review, your submission goes to the service hosting that form or review.

Permissions

  • storage saves your preferences and remembers whether you’ve responded to the feedback prompt.
  • offscreen keeps an ongoing recording running when the toolbar popup closes.
  • downloads saves a copy of a recording through Chrome’s download manager and checks whether the download finished.
  • Microphone access is requested through Chrome. It’s used when you start recording or run a mic test and can be revoked in Chrome settings. The extension does not request access to websites.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the Microphone Recorder support repository. Please do not post recordings or personal information there.
